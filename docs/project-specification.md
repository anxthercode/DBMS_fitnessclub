## 3. Functional scope and agreed rules

| Role | Required scope |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | Club information, trainers, configurable offers, schedule, first-visit information, sign-up/sign-in |
| Client | Profile, zone access, separately purchased personal training, cart, simulated checkout, order history, schedule, requests/cancellations and notifications |
| Trainer | Own schedule, create/edit slots, approve/reject requests and mark attendance/no-show |
| Administrator | Manage users, trainers, access/training offers, memberships, orders/payments and problematic requests; first-visit applications and pass redemption; discounts, rates, import/export, reports and analytics |

There are three account roles: CLIENT, TRAINER and ADMIN; a guest is not another database role. Clients register themselves, administrators create trainers, and a separate command creates the first administrator. Retain basic CRUD/archive, search/filter/sort, secure access, audit logging, RU/EN and in-app notifications. Email sending, verification and recovery are deferred. Limit JSON/XML/CSV import/export to access offers, Word/Excel/PDF generation to one sales report, and SQL analytics to four tabular summaries: sales, attendance, access-offer popularity and trainer occupancy. No generic report builder, automatic exports or chart dashboard.

### Simple account and administrator rules — selected 11 October

- Email is a unique login identifier; registration, purchases and bookings do not require email verification. Preserve Argon2id hashes, sessions, ownership/role checks and request protection. Do not fabricate `email_verified_at`. Disable mail calls and verification/recovery endpoints; remove related prompts/links from the ordinary UI. Existing token tables can remain unused; no destructive migration or replacement delivery system is needed.
- No public password recovery in this release. A local operator command may reset a forgotten password and revoke sessions; no administrator password-reset UI or external delivery. Authenticated users retain profile editing.
- Use ordinary administrator tables/forms: create/edit/archive trainers and access/training offers, activate/deactivate clients/trainers, inspect orders/payments/access and process visits. Keep the existing simple discount/rate model with basic forms. No staff permission designer or administrative role reassignment.
- Paid orders and issued access conditions are read-only; no manual free access, paid-membership cancellation, price rewriting, cash refunds or credit override screens. Existing historical cancelled records remain readable. Archiving offers does not invalidate paid purchases.
- Deactivating a client or trainer releases their future pending/approved bookings and reserved training units in the same transaction; preserve past records and issued access. Trainer deactivation also cancels future slots. Reactivation does not restore cancelled bookings automatically.

### Access products

- **Formats:** membership or single visit (`Разовое посещение` / `Single visit`). Select gym + cardio, pool, or both; at least one zone is required. Gym and cardio are one purchasable zone. Indoor/outdoor pool views represent the same pool zone.
- **Membership terms:** 1, 3 or 12 calendar months, with month-end clamping. The client chooses the start date in `Europe/Minsk`. Display the full-term total, with any monthly equivalent secondary. Each zone/term/format combination has a catalogue price; combined access need not equal the sum of separate prices. Current amounts are illustrative demo prices.
- **Included amenities:** changing rooms, lockers and showers, without an extra fee. Zone access covers self-directed exercise. Personal training is a separately purchased service; a membership alone does not pay for a trainer's time.
- **Membership overlap — approved 3 October 2026:** a client's non-cancelled periods may overlap only for disjoint zones. Combined access conflicts with either zone. Use `[starts_at, ends_at)`; adjacent periods are valid. Check existing access and all items in a purchase, and recheck at successful payment. Keep the selected date; never silently shift or automatically extend a period.
- **Single visit:** one admission on the selected date during opening hours. All purchased zones may be used within that visit; leaving ends it, with no re-entry. Purchase does not redeem a pass. An administrator finds the paid pass and marks it used at entry; store the redemption time and administrator identity and atomically reject a second use. A QR code is not required for v1. Unused passes expire at the end of their valid Minsk date; demo states are unused, used, expired and cancelled. A pass neither extends a membership nor qualifies for personal-training bookings. There is no client control that claims to verify physical admission; full check-in/check-out and occupancy management are outside the current scope.
- **Free first visit:** a separate self-directed gym/cardio/pool visit, with no personal training or repeat admission. The agreed application, eligibility and administrator workflow is specified below. HTTP mode stores the name/phone application for administrator review; the mock form remains a non-persistent demonstration.
- Do not add lane/machine/locker reservations, separate zone pages, a spa/shop catalogue or extra roles. Attendance reports cover scheduled sessions unless independent-visit reporting is separately agreed.

### Exercise modes and personal training — agreed 10 October 2026

- There are two modes: **self-directed exercise** and **personal training**. Use compact UI labels `Самостоятельно` / `С тренером` in Russian and `On your own` / `With a trainer` in English. Descriptive copy may use `Самостоятельные тренировки` / `Персональные тренировки` and `Self-directed workouts` / `Personal training`. Avoid calling self-directed exercise "individual training", which can be confused with one-to-one coaching.
- Self-directed exercise uses the purchased zone access and does not reserve a trainer or a scheduled session. A member can also buy personal training; the modes are not mutually exclusive account types.
- Personal training means **one client with one trainer**. The trainer chooses exercises, sets and repetitions and guides the session. This describes the service; a workout-program editor or exercise log is not part of the agreed software scope.
- Group training is outside the target scope. Every trainer slot has capacity one; remove group offerings, group-capacity controls and group/individual filters when updating the implementation.
- **Simple separate payment:** sell one 60-minute personal session per order line, for either gym/cardio or pool. Use an illustrative initial price of 30 BYN for either zone, editable for future purchases. No bundles, expiry, trainer categories, personal trainer assignment or package renewal. Multiple single-session purchases remain possible.
- A signed-in active client needs a non-cancelled membership covering the entire session and its zone, plus one unused paid session for that zone. Any available matching-zone trainer can be selected. A single-visit pass or free first visit does not replace the membership.
- Buy a session through the existing cart/payment flow first, then request a slot. Purchase does not reserve a time or guarantee availability. Paid units have no expiry in this coursework release; booking still requires a covering membership. No automatic membership extension or money refund.
- Cart zone editing updates the existing training line in place, with its ID, cart version and the explicitly reviewed offer price/revision. Cancellation leaves the cart unchanged. Payment success distinguishes training-only, access-only and mixed orders; training leads to time selection and access leads to issued records.

### Free first visit — agreed 10 October 2026

- Accept an application **without account registration**, using **name and phone number**. Email is not required. The form action is `Записаться на первый визит` / `Request your first visit`; explain that an administrator will contact the visitor to agree the date and access zones.
- Eligibility means **the person has never visited the club**, not merely that they have never claimed this offer. Check the normalized phone number against available visitor/client history and let the administrator make the final decision. A phone number alone does not prove identity or the absence of prior visits; do not claim complete automated detection when independent-visit history is incomplete.
- The administrator review card shows the application, matched accounts and expandable records from previous applications, passes, memberships and training bookings. Only used visits/passes and attended training are attendance evidence; purchases and no-shows are not. An empty result says that no attendance records were found, without asserting first-time eligibility. History is administrator-only, read from existing records without automatic identity merging or new history tables.
- The administrator reviews the application, agrees the date and zones with the visitor, confirms or rejects it, and records use at entry. Preserve who made each decision and when. Confirmation does not mean that the visit has already been used; redemption must prevent concurrent or repeated use.
- The offer covers one self-directed visit in the agreed zones, with no trainer session and no re-entry. It must not bypass membership or paid-training eligibility.
- Store applications with states pending, confirmed, rejected, cancelled and used; confirmed records have a Minsk visit date and chosen zones. Allow one pending/confirmed application per normalized phone. Reject repeat free-visit claims after a used record; allow a new application after rejection/cancellation. An administrator closes missed confirmations manually. Only a confirmed visit on its agreed date can be marked used, once. Record actor/time through existing audit logging; no automatic customer matching or account merging.
- Do not activate the public form until storage and administrator handling work. Phone contact is performed by the administrator; no SMS provider, automatic calling service or background scheduler.

The contact and terminology choices were informed by public examples reviewed on 10 October 2026: [World Class Irkutsk](https://worldclass-irk.ru/) uses name/phone for a club-tour request; [XFIT Heart of the Capital](https://www.xfit.ru/club/moskva/x-fit-serdtse-stolitsy-premium/) uses name/phone for a callback consultation; [X-Fit Pro](https://xfit-pro.ru/coach/panov/video/prezenterPanov) has a longer guest-visit form including email. [World Class's exercise-format article](https://worldclass-irk.ru/club/blog/samostoyatelnye-gruppovye-ili-personalnye-trenirovki/) distinguishes self-directed and personal training, and [XFIT's trainer service catalogue](https://www.xfit.ru/coaches/borisevich-andrey/) sells personal training individually and in packages. These are design references, not a requirement to copy their prices, forms or club policies.

### Cart, orders and simulated payment

The access workflow is **configure access → cart → immutable order → simulated payment → issued membership/pass**. Training uses that same flow, issuing one paid training unit per training line; booking is a separate action. Access and training lines may share a cart/order. No real bank integration, charges or monetary refunds.

- A cart line represents one configured membership/pass or one training session for one zone; checkout accepts at most 12 lines. Guest choices survive navigation and authentication and merge into the client's cart. If merging exceeds the limit, nothing is discarded: the client removes items before checkout. Training purchases have no selected date; the client selects a time later in the schedule.
- Validate zones, dates, active offers, expected prices and catalogue/cart versions. Changed prices require explicit review/update. Invalid promo codes block checkout. The demo promo is `NORTHSIDE10` (10%). Demo rates are USD = 3.25 BYN and EUR = 3.60 BYN; round converted totals to minor units and display the rate/date, BYN base and payment currency.
- Order creation clears purchased cart items but issues nothing. Snapshot products, zones, access dates, prices, discount, currency, rate and totals; training snapshots specify one 60-minute session in the chosen zone without expiry. Membership training-permission flags must not substitute for a separately purchased service. Later catalogue changes must not alter history. Use `NUMERIC` for persisted money and preserve financial records.
- Preserve declined payment attempts and allow retries. Duplicate submissions must not duplicate orders, payments or issuance. Successful payment issues access/paid sessions once and creates an in-app notification. Pending orders can be cancelled; paid orders cannot be paid/cancelled again. Recheck access dates and common-zone overlap at payment, including competing pending orders. A training-only purchase needs no current membership; its booking does.
- Buy-again links use current catalogue prices. Renewal preselects the same zones/term and the first selectable Minsk day after expiry (round a legacy non-midnight ending up), or today when already expired. The visible date remains editable and subject to overlap checks; historical access is never extended automatically.

### Schedule and bookings

The workflow is **buy a session → choose a slot → check membership, paid unit and availability → pending request → trainer decision → in-app notification**. Authentication preserves the selected slot but does not submit automatically. An unpaid client is directed to buy a session before requesting a slot; no temporary slot holds.

- An active signed-in client needs a non-cancelled membership covering the **entire session** and its zone, plus an unused paid unit for that zone. A future membership qualifies if it covers the session; single-visit passes never qualify. Email verification is not required.
- Every personal-training slot has capacity one. Pending and approved requests each reserve that place. Approval preserves the reservation; rejection/cancellation releases it. Prevent duplicate occupied bookings, overlapping trainer slots and overlapping pending/approved client bookings across **all** memberships, zones and trainers. Half-open intervals permit adjacent sessions. Recheck eligibility, state and availability on creation and approval under database transactions. Separately protect training entitlements from being allocated to multiple concurrent requests beyond the purchased count; place release and training-credit restoration are distinct operations.
- Display pending, approved, rejected, cancelled, attended and no-show outcomes. A pending/approved booking reserves one unit; attendance or approved-session no-show consumes it. Rejection, timely client cancellation and club cancellation release it. Terminal states cannot be overwritten. Clients cancel with a 1–500 character reason at or before exactly 12 hours before start; later self-cancellation is blocked. Trainer/admin club cancellation returns the unit without an exception/refund system.
- An already-started session cannot be approved retrospectively. Overdue pending requests appear in the trainer/admin list for manual rejection and unit release; no automatic expiry job or 12-hour escalation queue. Trainers mark attendance/no-show only for approved sessions after their end. Unapproved requests never consume a unit for no-show.
- Booking changes and successful payments create owned notifications with record links. Lists, capacities, unread counters and account overview must update consistently. The mock decision panel simulates only the signed-in client's request; HTTP mode uses the separate trainer/admin workspace and persisted notifications.
